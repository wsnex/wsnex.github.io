About WhatsApp Bulk Sender

WhatsApp Bulk Sender is a Windows app for businesses that need to reach their own customers quickly — schools sending fee reminders, stores sharing order updates, or shops announcing offers.

It is built to be used responsibly: send only to people who know your business. The official API mode uses your own WhatsApp Business number.

Made for: Schools, online stores, clinics, restaurants and any business messaging its own customers.
