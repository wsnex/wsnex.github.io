About School Management

School Management by WSNex is designed for the way Pakistani schools actually run — monthly fee cycles, class-wise attendance and exam result cards. It replaces registers and spreadsheets with one simple dashboard.

Pair it with the WSNex WhatsApp Bulk Sender to send fee reminders and notices to parents in English or Urdu.

Made for: Private schools, academies, coaching centres and school chains.
