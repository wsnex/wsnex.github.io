// FAQ

Questions people ask us

What does WSNex do?

WSNex is a software house in Gujranwala, Pakistan. We build restaurant POS, school management, WhatsApp automation, ISP & OLT management tools and custom software for businesses.

Can WSNex build a custom POS for my shop?

Yes. We build custom POS systems for pharmacies, grocery marts, retail and garment shops, repair shops, clinics, wholesalers and more — around the way your business already works.

Does the software work without internet?

Yes. Our apps like FoodDesk Pro keep working offline and sync automatically when the internet is back.

Do you support Urdu?

Yes. Our software supports English and Urdu, and we work with local payment methods like JazzCash and Easypaisa.

How can I contact WSNex?
