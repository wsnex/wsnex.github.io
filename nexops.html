About NexOps

NexOps was built by WSNex for real FTTH work, where stock leaves the store every day with different technicians. It keeps one clear record of what came in, who took what and what is left.

It pairs with our ISP & OLT management tools for customer billing control and OLT configuration.

Made for: Internet service providers, cable operators and FTTH contractors with field teams.

Questions

What does NexOps track?

Stock received, items issued to each technician and current stock levels, with low-stock alerts.

Does NexOps work on mobile?

Yes. Technicians can use the Android app, which also works offline and syncs later.

Does it handle prices and billing?

No. NexOps focuses only on stock management to keep it simple and fast.
