About Custom POS & Software

WSNex is a software house in Gujranwala. We start by understanding how your business runs day to day, sketch the screens with you, build and test the software, then install it and train your team.

Our software runs on Windows, the web and Android, works offline where needed, and supports Urdu and local payment methods like JazzCash and Easypaisa.

Made for: Shops and businesses in Gujranwala, Punjab and across Pakistan that need software built for their workflow.
