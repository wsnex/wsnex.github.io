About FoodDesk Pro

FoodDesk Pro was built after watching how busy a restaurant counter gets at peak time. Instead of a complicated system, it focuses on the things a counter actually does all day: taking orders quickly, keeping the kitchen informed and getting deliveries out the door.

It runs as a desktop app on your counter PC and in the browser, and staff phones can use it from Chrome. If the internet goes down, nothing stops — sales keep saving and sync automatically once you are back online.

Made for: Fast food outlets, pizza and burger shops, cafés, dhabas and multi-branch restaurant chains.

Questions

Does FoodDesk Pro work without internet?

Yes. It keeps working offline and syncs branch data automatically when the internet is back.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. FoodDesk Pro supports multiple branches, and you choose which menu products appear in each branch.

Can customers order online?

Yes, with the online ordering add-on. Each restaurant sets its own delivery fee and minimum order.
