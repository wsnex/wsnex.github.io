About Linkora

Linkora is an Android messenger built by WSNex around privacy. Instead of storing your conversations on a central server, it connects devices directly.

Made for: Friends, families and small teams who want a private way to chat.

Questions

Is Linkora available for iPhone?

Linkora is currently built for Android.

Does Linkora support groups?

Yes. The person who creates a group becomes its admin.

Can I send voice notes?

Yes, along with photos, files and view-once media.
