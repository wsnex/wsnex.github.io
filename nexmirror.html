About NexMirror

NexMirror is a lightweight Windows app from WSNex that puts your Android phone on your laptop screen over Wi-Fi.

The next version adds sharing in every direction — PC and Android, over the same Wi-Fi or the internet — using a unique code for each device, with view-only or full-control sessions.

Made for: Presenters, teachers, support teams, content creators and anyone who works on a laptop with a phone nearby.

Questions

Do I need a USB cable?

No. NexMirror works over Wi-Fi.

Can I control my phone from the laptop?

Yes, with your mouse and keyboard.

Will it support PC-to-PC sharing?

Yes, PC-to-PC and other directions are planned for the next version.
